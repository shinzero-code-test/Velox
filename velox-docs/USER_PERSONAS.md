# Velox User Personas

**App ID:** `com.exapps.velox`

---

## Persona 1 – Ahmed “The Everyday Viewer”

**Demographics**
- 22–28 years old
- Arabic primary language
- Mid-range Android phone
- Student or early-career

**Behaviors**
- Watches downloaded series and movies offline
- Listens to music and podcasts while commuting
- Uses MX Player or VLC out of habit
- Switches between video and audio frequently

**Needs**
- Fast, reliable playback of common formats
- Good subtitle support (Arabic + English)
- Clean interface that doesn’t feel outdated
- Simple library browsing

**Frustrations**
- Cluttered or English-centric UIs
- Poor Arabic text rendering
- Ads or unnecessary account prompts
- Slow app or awkward gestures

**Velox value**
- Beautiful Arabic-first experience
- Reliable local playback
- Pleasant dark glass interface

---

## Persona 2 – Sara “The Music-First User”

**Demographics**
- 18–26 years old
- Strong preference for Arabic UI
- Uses phone as main music device

**Behaviors**
- Large local music collection
- Creates and maintains playlists
- Cares about album art and lyrics
- Uses equalizer and sleep timer

**Needs**
- Attractive Now Playing screen
- Easy playlist management
- Good audio effects
- Smooth, modern feel

**Frustrations**
- Music players that feel cheap or childish
- Weak Arabic support
- Limited customization of sound

**Velox value**
- Large artwork + glass aesthetic
- Solid EQ and playback features
- Arabic-first design

---

## Persona 3 – Omar “The Power User”

**Demographics**
- 25–40 years old
- Tech-comfortable
- May use tablets or higher-end phones

**Behaviors**
- Plays rare formats and high-bitrate files
- Uses network shares or external storage
- Tweaks gestures, decoder settings, subtitles timing
- Expects precision and control

**Needs**
- Deep playback options
- Network streaming (later)
- Accurate seeking and track selection
- Performance on large libraries

**Frustrations**
- Beautiful players that lack power
- Powerful players with terrible UX
- Missing advanced features

**Velox value**
- Roadmap toward power features
- Clean access to advanced controls
- No sacrifice of core performance

---

## Persona 4 – Layla “The Family / Shared Device User”

**Demographics**
- 30–45 years old
- Shares phone/tablet with family sometimes
- Values simplicity and stability

**Behaviors**
- Plays kids’ content, series, Quran audio, music
- Wants things to “just work”
- Rarely digs into settings

**Needs**
- Stable, predictable behavior
- Clear Arabic interface
- Easy resume and library access

**Frustrations**
- Complicated menus
- App crashes or format failures
- Confusing English-only options

**Velox value**
- Calm default experience
- Reliable core features
- High-quality Arabic language

---

## Common Insights Across Personas

1. Arabic language quality and RTL are not “nice to have” — they are table stakes for trust.
2. Visual modernity is a major differentiator against entrenched players.
3. Users accept progressive feature addition if the core experience is excellent.
4. Privacy and lack of forced accounts are appreciated, especially in the region.
5. Performance on mid-range devices matters more than flagship-only polish.
